BEFORE YOU BUY

Good to know.

What exactly am I buying?

A one-time digital download with AI workflow prompts and setup guidance for the free GTA Auto Clipper.

Is this a subscription?

No. The pack costs ₹149 once. The clipper is free and can be used without buying the guide.

Will it automatically search and post?

Yes, after one-time setup. Your AI runner uses YouTube search to discover live streams, finds gameplay moments, runs the clipper and schedules finished posts through Zernio. Keep the computer awake and online while it searches and clips; Zernio can publish scheduled posts while it sleeps.

Can it send automatic DMs?

Optional Zernio workflows can automate eligible Instagram inbox replies or comment-to-DM flows after you connect and authorize the account. This is for supported inbound conversations and user-triggered flows, not unsolicited bulk DMs. YouTube does not have a direct-message feature.

Does the clipper use chat?

By default it selects moments using chat reactions. If chat is unavailable or quiet, it falls back to audio and speech. You can also choose to combine all signals or use audio and speech only.

Do I need permission to clip a creator?

Yes. You are responsible for having permission or another clear legal right to record, edit and publish footage.

Is this an official Rockstar product?

No. GTA Auto Clipper is an independent fan-made project, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
