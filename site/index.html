Will the free tool post for me?

No. The free tool makes clips for you to review. The optional playbook explains how to set up scheduled posting on your own accounts.

Can it help find GTA V creators?

Yes. It can help you find creators who are live. Choose creators whose videos you have permission to use.

Can I clip any creator?

Only clip videos you have permission to use. The original creator still owns their content.
