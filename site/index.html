GOOD TO KNOW

Before you press play.

Does the free tool upload anything?

No. The clipper renders locally and saves files for review. Posting is a separate Zernio workflow you configure with your own connected accounts.

Can it pick the biggest live GTA 6 channel?

It can search live results and use YouTube’s view-count ordering to surface candidates. It cannot promise an accurate current concurrent-viewer ranking.

Can I clip any streamer?

The channel allowlist is optional and removed from this release. You are responsible for having the rights and permissions needed to record, edit, and publish source footage.

Is this an official GTA product?

No. GTA Auto Clipper is an independent fan-made utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, YouTube, Instagram, or Zernio.
